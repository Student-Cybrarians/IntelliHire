# IntelliHire

**AI-powered career preparation and placement training platform for students and job seekers.**

IntelliHire brings resume intelligence, adaptive assessment, technical interview practice, and candidate readiness into one workflow.

## What it does

- **Resume & JD analysis** — analyze ATS alignment, skills, gaps, and preparation needs.
- **Adaptive assessments** — practice assessments with persisted attempts, answers, and scoring.
- **Technical interview practice** — structured technical interview sessions with evaluation and completion reporting.
- **Career intelligence** — turn resume and job-description evidence into skill-gap and preparation insights.
- **Production-minded architecture** — authentication, persistence, AI-provider handling, testing, and audit-oriented design.

## Scope

IntelliHire is designed for **candidate preparation and skill development**. It is not intended to make autonomous hiring or rejection decisions.

This repository is the **canonical IntelliHire project**. The versioned IntelliHire repositories in the organization represent earlier implementation iterations rather than separate portfolio products.
