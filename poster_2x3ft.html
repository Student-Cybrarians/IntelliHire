Abstract Scope & Contribution

Conventional recruitment heavily depends on opaque keyword-matching Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and uncalibrated human interviews, frequently resulting in keyword gaming, demographic bias, and an absence of candidate diagnostic feedback. IntelliHire is an intelligent recruitment training and talent intelligence platform structured into five sequential, evidence-grounded modules: Module 1 (Resume Intelligence, ATS & Job Description Matching), Module 2 (Adaptive Learning, Assessment & Interview Preparation), Module 3 (Technical, Domain & Professional Simulation), Module 4 (Behavioral, Communication & Professional Interaction Simulation), and Module 5 (Candidate Readiness & Human Decision Support). Powered by an edge-native serverless architecture and verified across 523 automated tests, IntelliHire anchors all evaluations in verified candidate claims, provides adaptive misconception remediation, enforces zero biometric facial emotion scoring, and preserves human decision sovereignty.

Core Progression: Resume & JD ➔ Diagnostics ➔ Adaptive Learn ➔ Simulation ➔ Voice AI Mock ➔ Readiness
Problem Statement Industry Challenges
  • ATS Keyword Gaming & Inaccurate Filtering (Module 1): Static heuristic keyword filters reject qualified applicants due to minor syntax or layout variations while incentivizing prompt injection and keyword stuffing.
  • The "Teach-Test" Disconnect & Missing Feedback (Module 2): Rejected candidates receive uninformative rejection notices without diagnostic feedback or remediation of underlying conceptual misconceptions.
  • Trivia Quizzes vs Realistic Execution (Module 3): Traditional screening relies on abstract puzzles rather than realistic domain tasks that evaluate architecture tradeoffs, database queries, and debugging.
  • Uncalibrated Interviews & Biometric Surveillance (Module 4 & 5): Subjective interviewer bias and controversial facial emotion AI compromise candidate fairness without transparent rubric calibration.
Objectives Modular Design Goals
  • Objective 1 (Module 1 — Resume Intelligence): Develop an in-process, PII-redacted multi-pass parser aligning resume evidence to job descriptions using neural embeddings (bge-base-en-v1.5).
  • Objective 2 (Module 2 — Adaptive Learning): Formulate a Bayesian skill proficiency engine with teach-before-testing curricula that dynamically updates mastery uncertainty intervals.
  • Objective 3 (Module 3 — Technical Simulation): Implement role-aligned task workspaces to observe practical code execution, database queries, and system design tradeoffs.
  • Objective 4 (Module 4 — Interaction Simulation): Build dual-mode interviews: a 10-turn Voice AI simulation with adaptive probing alongside a WebRTC live human room.
  • Objective 5 (Module 5 — Readiness & Decision Support): Synthesize multi-module evidence into calibrated 1–5 rubrics and candidate dossiers with zero biometric profiling.
Methodology & System Framework — End-to-End Five-Module Architecture Cloudflare Edge-Native Serverless Implementation
Tier 1 Ingestion & Claims Grounding

Module 1: Ingests candidate resumes (PDF/DOCX/TeX) and target JDs. Deterministically strips sensitive PII before entity extraction to prevent model demographic bias. BGE-Base neural embeddings align extracted skills to standardized O*NET/ESCO taxonomies with cryptographic provenance stored in Cloudflare D1.

Tier 2 Adaptive Learning & Simulation

Modules 2, 3 & 4: Module 2 computes Bayesian skill proficiency and targets diagnosed misconceptions with teach-before-testing units. Module 3 evaluates practical code and query tasks. Module 4 synthesizes a 10-turn blueprint across 10 competency dimensions, executing real-time voice interviews and WebRTC human rooms.

Tier 3 Readiness & Decision Support

Module 5: Consolidates multi-module evidence into candidate coaching feedback and calibrated recruiter dossiers. All evaluations are anchored to 1–5 behavioral rubrics (Unsatisfactory, Competent, Exceptional). Strict human sovereignty guarantees that AI acts solely as evidence synthesis without autonomous hire/reject rulings.

Practical Applications Deployment Targets
  • University Campus Placement Preparation: Enables continuous, autonomous interview training and diagnostic skill gap remediation for engineering students prior to competitive placement drives.
  • Calibrated Technical Hiring Workflows: Equips hiring managers with evidence-based interview kits and standardized 1–5 anchored rubrics, mitigating interviewer fatigue and halo effects.
  • Regulated & High-Stakes Skill Verification: Provides defensible, auditable candidate evidence packages in cloud systems, finance, and healthcare with tamper-resistant provenance logs.
Conclusion & Contribution Research Impact
"IntelliHire demonstrates that transitioning from speculative keyword-based screening to an evidence-grounded, five-module training architecture substantially enhances candidate preparation while upholding human decision sovereignty. By combining deterministic PII redaction, Bayesian skill mastery, practical work simulations, calibrated Voice AI interviews, and non-decisional readiness dossiers, the platform establishes an ethical, transparent, and technically rigorous foundation for recruitment training and candidate evaluation."
